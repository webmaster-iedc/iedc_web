Our Vision

To establish IEDC CET as a centre of excellence in technology-driven entrepreneurship, fostering a sustainable ecosystem that empowers students to become innovators, founders, and industry leaders — integrating cutting-edge technologies, industry collaborations, and startup support.

02

Our Mission

Promote an innovation-driven entrepreneurial culture among students through structured mentorship and hands-on experience.
Foster enterprise among budding technopreneurs and create jobs by nurturing high-potential student startups.
Bridge the gap between industry and academia through strategic collaborations and knowledge-sharing platforms.
03

Our Objectives

Facilitate experiential learning through workshops and mentorship aligned with industry processes.
Establish strong partnerships with industry leaders, research units, and global startup enablers.
Support development of market-ready products with robust prototyping and acceleration setups.
